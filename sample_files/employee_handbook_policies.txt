INDEX CORP — ACADEMIC SERVICES DIVISION
EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK & OPERATIONAL POLICIES (2024-2025)

SECTION 1: REMOTE WORK AND HYBRID ARRANGEMENTS
Team members in engineering and research roles are eligible for up to three (3) days of remote work per workweek. Core synchronous collaboration hours are maintained between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM EST.

SECTION 2: TRAVEL & EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
All business-related expenses must be submitted within thirty (30) calendar days of accrual. Meal allowances during overnight academic conferences are capped at $75 per diem. Receipts are mandatory for all transactions exceeding $25.

SECTION 3: ANNUAL LEAVE AND SICK QUOTAS
Full-time personnel receive twenty-two (22) days of paid time off (PTO) annually, accruing on the first day of each month. A maximum of five (5) unused PTO days may be rolled over into the subsequent calendar year.

SECTION 4: PROFESSIONAL DEVELOPMENT
Each full-time researcher receives an annual continuous education stipend of $2,000 for verified textbooks, research publications, certifications, and technical symposiums.
